# Wassup

A global news intelligence system. Wassup collects news and social media from around the world, filters it against a learned interest profile, lets a newsroom of AI agents (run by [Paperclip](https://github.com/paperclipai/paperclip)) research and connect stories, and shows everything on an interactive globe and evidence board.

See [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md) for the full design and roadmap.

## Status
